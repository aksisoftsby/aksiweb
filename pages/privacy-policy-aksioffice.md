Effective date: October 29, 2026

Aksisoft Media respects the privacy of users of the **Aksi Office** app. This page explains, briefly and completely, how the app handles your data. Our general privacy policy for all of our apps is available at [aksisoft.web.id/privacy-policy](/privacy-policy/).

## Summary

- Aksi Office is a **local office document app** (reader/editor/creator for Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF files). All document processing — viewing, editing, creating, converting, merging, splitting, compressing, e-signing, and OCR scanning — happens **entirely on your device**.
- Your documents are **never uploaded to our servers**; we do not operate any document storage service.
- We **have no user accounts** in the app and we **do not collect, store, or sell** your personal data.
- The app shows **ads from Google AdMob** (banner and native advanced formats).

## Information the app accesses

The app reads documents, spreadsheets, presentations, PDFs, and images that are already on your device in order to open and work with them. That data:

- is processed **only on your device**;
- is **never sent to our servers**;
- is only sent over the network when **you choose** to (for example, using the Share feature to send a file to an app of your choice).

## Documents and tools

- **Word / Excel / PPT / PDF editors:** your documents are opened, modified, and saved on the device. Exports are written to storage on your device, where you remain in full control.
- **PDF tools (merge, split, compress, e-sign, watermark, extract text):** performed locally in the app.
- **OCR scanner & camera-based features:** the camera is used **only while scanning** a page with the scanner tool. Scanning runs on-device; no photos are uploaded anywhere.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads from **Google AdMob** (banner and native advanced ads). To serve ads:

- the app requests the **INTERNET** permission;
- Google may collect and use certain data (for example, an anonymous device/ad identifier, approximate coarse location such as general country area, and information about the app) to serve and measure ads, as described in [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and in the [AdMob advertising policy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543);
- you can limit personalized advertising across all apps in **Google Settings → Ads**, or by using your device's **Reset advertising ID** option.

Content **inside your documents is never used for advertising**. Ads may be shown on dedicated ad surfaces (bottom banner on the main screens and in editors, and native ad cards in file lists) that are clearly labeled as advertising.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** only for serving AdMob advertising and related Google services.
- **Storage / document access:** to open and save the files you pick yourself. The app never reads files without your choice.

## Children's privacy

Aksi Office is a general-audience productivity tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Ads are not configured in a child-directed format.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page with a new effective date.
