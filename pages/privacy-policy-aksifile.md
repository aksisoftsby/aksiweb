Effective date: October 27, 2026

Aksisoft Media respects the privacy of users of the **Aksi File Manager** app. This page explains, briefly and completely, how the app handles your data. Our general privacy policy for all of our apps is available at [aksisoft.web.id/privacy-policy](/privacy-policy/).

## Summary

- Aksi File Manager is a **local file manager**. All file operations (browsing, copying, moving, renaming, compressing, editing, converting, viewing) happen **entirely on your device**. Your files are **never uploaded to our servers** — we do not operate any file/photos/media storage server.
- We **have no user accounts** in the app and we **do not collect, store, or sell** your personal data.
- The app shows **ads from Google AdMob** (banner and native advanced formats).
- The optional FTP/SFTP client connects **only to the server address you enter yourself**; it is not a cloud service and data is not transferred anywhere else by us.
- The optional PIN/App lock stores a **hash of the PIN locally on your device** (encrypted using the Android Keystore). We never see it.

## Information the app accesses

The app reads files and media that are already on your device in order to display and manage them. That data:

- is processed **only on your device**;
- is **never sent to our servers**;
- is only sent over the network when **you choose** to (for example, using the FTP/SFTP client to a server you configured, or the Share/Open-with feature to apps you choose).

## Files, media, and tools

- **Storage Analyzer / Cleaner / Duplicates:** scanning is performed locally to calculate storage usage, find junk files, and detect duplicate files. Results are shown only to you.
- **Editor / Image editor / PDF tools / Office viewers:** your documents and images are opened and modified on the device; exports are saved in storage on your device where you can control them.
- **QR scanner:** the camera is used **only while scanning** a QR/Barcode to decode and open content you choose. No photos or identification data are stored or transmitted.
- **Downloads / URL fetch:** only when you ask the app to download a file from a URL you enter.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads from **Google AdMob** (banner and native advanced ads). To serve ads:

- the app requests the **INTERNET** permission;
- Google may use the **Android Advertising ID** and limited technical data to serve relevant ads, in accordance with the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and [Google Ads settings](https://adssettings.google.com);
- files, folders, and other content on your device are **not used** to select ads;
- you can opt out of ad personalization through your Android system settings, and you can disable ads from Settings > Ads in the app.

We do not serve adult-content ads and we do not request sensitive user data.

## Permissions requested

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | Reading and writing files (legacy Android versions) |
| `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | Full "All files access" so the file manager can browse and manage files on Android 11+ |
| `INTERNET` | Displaying AdMob ads; optional FTP/SFTP connections that you configure |
| `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` | Opening the installer for APK files that already exist on your device |
| `REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES` | Uninstalling apps that you choose to remove |
| `CAMERA` | Scanning QR codes and barcodes (used only while you are scanning) |

The app does **not** request location, contacts, microphone, or SMS permissions.

## Children

The app is intended for **general users** (managing files on their own device), it is not designed specifically for children, and it does not collect personal data from anyone.
